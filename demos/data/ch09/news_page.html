Uttar Pradesh leaves the Taj Mahal out of its tourism booklet

By a staff reporter, 8 October 2017

NEW DELHI: The government of the Indian state of Uttar Pradesh has drawn criticism for leaving the Taj Mahal out of a new tourism booklet released on 2 October.

The white marble mausoleum in Agra, which the Mughal emperor Shah Jahan built in memory of his wife Mumtaz Mahal, is a UNESCO World Heritage Site and draws millions of visitors every year. The 32-page booklet does not mention it and highlights other sites in the state instead.

Tour operators in Agra expect no drop in visitors: the domes and minarets of the monument remain among the most photographed sights in the world.
